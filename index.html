More answers in our FAQ

Does 9 Arrow price land clearing by the acre or by the day?

9 Arrow shows land clearing estimates by the day or by the acre. The number comes after a discovery call, a site visit or both, once the crew understands the vegetation, the terrain and the access. Density and terrain change how fast a machine moves, so the estimate follows the land itself.

What affects the cost of clearing an acre of land?

The cost of clearing an acre depends on vegetation density, tree size and species, topography, access, maneuverability and soil conditions. Thick growth on steep, rocky ground takes longer than light brush on flat ground. A tree that takes an hour on a smaller machine can take as little as 15 minutes on a large one.

What is forestry mulching?

Forestry mulching is a land clearing method that grinds trees, brush and vegetation where they stand. 9 Arrow's forestry mulching handles light regrowth, dense brush, large hardwoods and mature trees with no pushing, piling, hauling or burning. The ground is left covered in fine mulch, and the soil stays intact.

Does 9 Arrow remove stumps and root balls?

9 Arrow leaves root balls in place during forestry mulching because they keep the soil stable. When full removal is needed, such as under a future foundation, the crew grubs with bulldozers and excavators. The pulled root balls are then mulched on site, which 9 Arrow prefers over hauling them away.

Can 9 Arrow get rid of old brush piles?

Yes. 9 Arrow mulches existing brush piles on site, including old bulldozer piles as large as a house. Mulching replaces burning, which is often prohibited during Texas burn bans. The pile is ground into fine mulch on the property, so nothing has to be loaded, hauled off or burned.
