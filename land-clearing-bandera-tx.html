Land Clearing · Bandera, TX

Land Clearing in Bandera, Texas

Need land clearing in Bandera, TX? 9 Arrow Land Service brings forestry mulching, cedar removal, and ranch clearing to the Cowboy Capital and all of Bandera County. We're a family-owned Hill Country crew — fast, accurate, and known for the best finish in the business.

Bandera County is real ranch country — steep cedar-choked hillsides, Medina River bottoms, and pastures that have been losing ground to juniper for decades. We reclaim grazing land, cut in hunting lanes and food plots, and open up the long views that make this country worth owning.

What we do in Bandera

  • Forestry mulching & full land clearing
  • Lot, fence-line & building-site clearing
  • Cedar, mesquite & brush removal
  • Survey-guided clearing to your property lines
  • No burn piles — mulched in place

Why Bandera landowners choose 9 Arrow

We mulch in place — no burn piles, no hauling — and price every job by how many hours it takes, so you know the cost before we start. From a hunting tract off FM 470 to full pasture reclamation, we treat your Bandera County ranch like our own. It does not get better.

Frequently asked

Do you reclaim overgrown pasture in Bandera County?

Yes — mulching knocks cedar and brush back to grass, and the mulch layer feeds the soil as it breaks down.

Can you clear hunting land without tearing it up?

Yes. We cut lanes, plots, and access trails cleanly, keep the cover you want, and leave no burn piles behind.
