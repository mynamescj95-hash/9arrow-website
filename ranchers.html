FAQs

Frequently asked

How do I clear cedar and mesquite off my Texas ranch?

9 Arrow mulches cedar, mesquite, and brush in place with high-horsepower forestry equipment — no burn piles, no hauling — reopening your pasture and sight lines.

How much does cedar removal cost in Texas?

Cedar and mesquite clearing is priced by how many hours an area takes — hourly, daily, or by the acre — and depends on density, size, terrain, and access. We give you a number up front.

Does clearing cedar bring back grass and water?

Yes. Cedar is a heavy water user that shades out native grass. Removing it returns groundwater and reopens pasture for grazing, hay, and wildlife.

Can you clear brush without burning it?

Absolutely. We grind brush into mulch on site — no burn piles and no waiting on burn bans.

Who does ranch land clearing and pasture restoration near me in Central Texas?

9 Arrow Land Service, based in Spring Branch, serves ranches across Bulverde, New Braunfels, Boerne, the Hill Country, and Central Texas — selectively clearing brush while protecting your best oaks.
