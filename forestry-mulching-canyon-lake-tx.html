Forestry Mulching in Canyon Lake, Texas

Need forestry mulching in Canyon Lake, TX? 9 Arrow Land Service provides high-production forestry mulching in Canyon Lake and across the Texas Hill Country. We're a family-owned crew based nearby in Spring Branch — fast, accurate, and known for the best finish in the business.

On Canyon Lake's wooded, sloped lots, forestry mulching is the cleanest way to clear — grinding cedar and brush in place without piling or burning on tight properties.

What we do in Canyon Lake

  • High-production forestry mulching
  • Cedar, mesquite & brush ground in place
  • Selective clearing — keep your best trees
  • Clean, drivable mulch finish
  • No burn piles, no hauling

Why Canyon Lake landowners choose 9 Arrow

We mulch in place — no burn piles, no hauling — and price every job by how many hours it takes, so you know the cost before we start. From a single overgrown lot to large acreage, we treat your Canyon Lake-area property like our own. It does not get better.

Frequently asked

Is mulching good for lake lots?

Yes — it's ideal for wooded, sloped lots where burning and hauling are impractical.

Yes, we selectively mulch to open sight lines and views.
