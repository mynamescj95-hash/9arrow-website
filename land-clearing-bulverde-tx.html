What does land clearing in Bulverde, TX involve?

Land clearing in Bulverde, TX usually means grinding cedar, brush and unwanted trees where they stand while keeping the live oaks worth saving. 9 Arrow Land Service uses forestry mulchers that leave a fine finish you can walk and drive on. There are no burn piles to manage and nothing to haul away.

The Bulverde Market Day grounds, a year and a half later

The Bulverde job we can point to by name is the 20 acres where Bulverde Market Day is held. Ashley Cooke left a five-star Google review about the work.

"They cleared our 20 acres in Bulverde where Bulverde Market Day is held and did a great job! A year and a half later the land looks incredible. They are so easy to work with and have great communication (which is hard to find!)!"

Event grounds take foot traffic, vendor setups and vehicles. A fine mulch finish, the small chips some people call toothpicks, is easier to drive over than chunky debris and lets grass come back faster. We don't consider a job finished unless we're proud of the result.

Land clearing in Bulverde, between the Hill Country and San Antonio

Keeping cleared Bulverde land looking right

Cleared land in Bulverde keeps growing, and regrowth is easier to manage early. In the Texas growing season, monthly mowing is ideal, and every 2 to 3 months is the outer limit during peak growth. One final mow usually carries a property through winter.

Our grounds maintenance work covers mowing, weed eating, debris cleanup and selective trimming with chainsaws. Recurring agreements are available for residential property, and rural and ranch property is scheduled by season and conditions.

Getting Bulverde land ready to sell or build

Raw Bulverde land under a wall of cedar hides what a buyer is paying for. Clearing the understory, opening a drivable path and leaving the best live oaks lets people see the lay of the land, the views and the likely building spots.

Properly cleared and maintained land is healthier, more valuable and more enjoyable to own. On a Bulverde tract headed to market, the goal is ground that shows well the day it is listed and still looks right a season later. We don't just clear land. We stage property.

How long does it take to clear 20 acres in Bulverde?

Clearing 20 acres in Bulverde depends on density and terrain. Smaller machines clear about half an acre to 1 acre per 8-hour day. Large, high-horsepower machines run about 1 to 3 acres a day with reasonable tree coverage, so 20 acres on a big machine works out to roughly 7 to 20 working days.

Is land clearing in Bulverde priced by the acre?

Land clearing in Bulverde is estimated by the day or by the acre, whichever fits the job. The number comes after a discovery call or site visit, once we have seen density, tree size and species, topography, access, maneuverability and soil conditions. Those factors swing widely from one Bulverde tract to the next.

Will Bulverde pasture still be usable after mulching?

Bulverde pasture stays usable after forestry mulching because the soil is left intact and the finish is fine enough to drive over. Under a light mulch layer, grass can come back within days after rain. Dozing disturbs the soil instead, which raises erosion risk and slows recovery on Hill Country ground.
