Brush & Underbrush Removal

Brush & underbrush removal

Proudly Serving Central Texas

Serving Spring Branch, Bulverde, New Braunfels, Canyon Lake, Boerne, San Antonio, San Marcos, the Texas Hill Country, and clients across Central Texas.

Need brush clearing in Texas? 9 Arrow mulches overgrown brush and underbrush in place across Central Texas — clearing choked, unusable lots into clean, open, walkable ground without burn piles or hauling.

  • Mulches brush & underbrush in a single pass
  • Reclaims overgrown lots and reopens sight lines
  • Creates defensible space around homes & structures
  • No burn piles, no hauling, no mess
  • Light enough for residential lots, strong enough for ranches

Reclaim an overgrown lot

Years of neglected growth turn usable land into a liability — fire risk, hidden hazards, and zero curb appeal. We grind it back to clean ground so you can see, walk, and use your property again.

Brush mulching vs. burning

Burning brush is slow and often banned during dry seasons. Brush mulching is faster, safer, and leaves mulch that protects the soil. See our guide to forestry mulching.

FAQs

Frequently asked

How much does brush removal cost in Texas?

Brush clearing is priced by how many hours an area takes — hourly, daily, or by the acre — depending on density and access. We give you a number up front.

Can you clear an overgrown lot without burning?

Yes. We mulch brush and underbrush in place, so there are no burn piles and no waiting on burn bans.

Do you create defensible space?

We clear and thin brush to create defensible space around homes and structures, reducing wildfire fuel.
