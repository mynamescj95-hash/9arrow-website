Site Prep & Building Pads

Land clearing & site prep

Proudly Serving Central Texas

Serving Spring Branch, Bulverde, New Braunfels, Canyon Lake, Boerne, San Antonio, San Marcos, the Texas Hill Country, and clients across Central Texas.

Planning to build? Land clearing for a building site is step one. 9 Arrow clears, grades, and shapes home sites, pads, and construction sites across Central Texas — delivering a clean, level, construction-ready surface so your build starts on solid ground.

  • Clearing & grubbing for home sites and pads
  • Building pad clearing and fine grading
  • Drainage shaping and access roads
  • Survey-guided clearing to your site plan
  • Debris-free, construction-ready finish

From raw land to building pad

We open access, clear to your plan, shape the pad, and manage drainage — so your foundation, utility, and framing crews step onto a site that's ready. No rework, no cleanup delays.

Clearing land to build or sell

Whether you're prepping a home site or staging raw land, a clean, gradable site shows better and builds faster. See our land clearing cost guide for planning.

FAQs

Frequently asked

What does land clearing for a building site cost?

Site prep is priced by scope and hours — clearing, grubbing, grading, and access — after we review your site plan. You'll know the cost before we start.

Do you grade building pads?

Yes. We clear, grub, and fine-grade building and equipment pads to the elevations and slopes your plans require.

Can you prep a home site in the Hill Country?

Yes — home sites, pads, and construction sites across Spring Branch, Bulverde, Boerne, and Central Texas.
