What happens after you request a land clearing estimate?

After you request a land clearing estimate, 9 Arrow Land Service sets up a discovery call about the property and your goal, then visits the site when needed. The estimate reflects density, tree size and species, topography, access, maneuverability and soil conditions, and it is priced by the day or by the acre.

What a land clearing estimate is based on

Every 9 Arrow land clearing estimate is built on six factors.

What to share in an estimate request

A useful 9 Arrow estimate request covers where the property is, roughly how many acres, what is growing on it and what you want the land to become. Developers and utility partners with survey files can mention them for survey line clearing.

How clearing pace shapes an estimate

Clearing pace matters when 9 Arrow prices by the day. Our smaller machines clear about half an acre to 1 acre per 8-hour day, and larger machines about 1 to 3 acres with reasonable tree coverage. Our forestry mulching cost per acre guide covers cost factors in more detail.

Common questions

More answers in our FAQ

Can I get a land clearing price over the phone?

9 Arrow prices land clearing after a discovery call, a site visit or both. The discovery call happens by phone, and for some jobs that conversation is enough to build an estimate. Other jobs need a site visit so the crew can see the land firsthand.

What if I am not sure which service I need?

If you are not sure which service you need, choose "Not sure yet" on the form and describe the land and your goal. 9 Arrow works out the right mix on the discovery call, whether that means forestry mulching, roads, survey line clearing, site work or grounds maintenance.

Does 9 Arrow give estimates outside Spring Branch?

Yes. 9 Arrow is based in Spring Branch and comes to your land across Central Texas and the Texas Hill Country. Include the property location in your request so we can plan the discovery call and any site visit. Our service areas page lists the towns we cover.
