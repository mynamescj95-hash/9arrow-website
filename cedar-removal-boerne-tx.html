Cedar Removal in Boerne, Texas

Need cedar removal in Boerne, TX? 9 Arrow Land Service provides cedar & Ashe juniper removal in Boerne and across the Texas Hill Country. We're a family-owned crew based nearby in Spring Branch — fast, accurate, and known for the best finish in the business.

Boerne's Hill Country ranches are often overrun with Ashe juniper. We remove cedar selectively to return groundwater and grass while protecting the oaks and views that make Boerne land special.

What we do in Boerne

  • Cedar & Ashe juniper mulched in place
  • Reopen pasture & return groundwater
  • Selective clearing around live oaks
  • Dense cedar brakes cleared fast
  • Ongoing maintenance available

Why Boerne landowners choose 9 Arrow

We mulch in place — no burn piles, no hauling — and price every job by how many hours it takes, so you know the cost before we start. From a single overgrown lot to large acreage, we treat your Boerne-area property like our own. It does not get better.

Frequently asked

Why remove cedar in Boerne?

Cedar drinks water and crowds out grass; removing it restores pasture, water, and Hill Country views.

Do you protect oaks during cedar removal?

Yes — selective clearing keeps your live oaks while taking the cedar.
