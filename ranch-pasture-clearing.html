Ranch & Pasture Clearing

Ranch & pasture clearing

Proudly Serving Central Texas

Serving Spring Branch, Bulverde, New Braunfels, Canyon Lake, Boerne, San Antonio, San Marcos, the Texas Hill Country, and clients across Central Texas.

Ranch land clearing in Texas is about getting your ground back. 9 Arrow clears cedar, mesquite, and brush and restores pasture for grazing and hay across the Hill Country — turning choked, overgrown acreage into clean, productive, open range.

  • Cedar & mesquite removal that returns water to the land
  • Pasture reclamation for grazing and hay
  • Clean fence lines, sight lines & cattle access
  • Selective clearing that protects your shade trees
  • Ongoing mowing to keep pasture open

Reclaim your pasture

Heavy brush steals water, grass, and usable acreage from your herd. We mulch it in place and leave a clean finish that lets native grass return — opening your ranch back up for cattle, wildlife, and recreation.

Pasture reclamation, done right

We're lifelong Texans who treat your land like our own. From the front gate to the back fence, we help your place work the way a ranch should. Read our cedar & mesquite ranch guide.

FAQs

Frequently asked

How do you clear cattle pasture?

We mulch cedar, mesquite, and brush in place with high-horsepower equipment — no burning — reopening grazing and improving water availability.

Can you restore an overgrown pasture?

Yes. Pasture reclamation is one of our most common ranch jobs — we clear the brush, protect your best trees, and leave clean, grazeable ground.

Do you serve ranches across the Hill Country?

Yes — Spring Branch, Bulverde, Blanco, Boerne, Canyon Lake, and the wider Texas Hill Country.
