Who We Serve

Right-of-Way Clearing for Energy & Utilities

Proudly Serving Central Texas

Serving Spring Branch, Bulverde, New Braunfels, Canyon Lake, Boerne, San Antonio, San Marcos, the Texas Hill Country, and clients across Central Texas.

Linear infrastructure demands accuracy, coordination, and crews that can cover long distances across many properties. 9 Arrow opens and maintains right-of-way corridors for energy and utility clients across Texas.

  • Pipeline & power-line corridors, often 90ft+ wide
  • Survey-integrated clearing that stays inside the easement
  • First contractor on site, ahead of grading & trenching
  • Professional multi-landowner coordination
  • Ongoing ROW maintenance to stay compliant

Corridors, done right

We use forestry mulching to open wide corridors across routes that can run for hundreds of miles, loading your surveyor's files directly onto our machines so we clear accurately and without deviation — keeping downstream crews on schedule.

Maintained for the long haul

Once an easement is established, vegetation regrows. We keep gas, power, and water corridors accessible, operable, and compliant year over year.

FAQs

Frequently asked

Who does right-of-way clearing for pipelines and power lines in Texas?

9 Arrow opens and maintains ROW corridors for energy and utility clients statewide — survey-integrated clearing, first crew on site, and ongoing maintenance.

How wide of a utility corridor can you clear?

We routinely open corridors of 90 feet or more across routes that can run for hundreds of miles.

Do you clear right-of-way to survey and easement data?

Yes. We load the surveyor's files onto our machines and clear accurately, staying inside the easement and minimizing disturbance.

Do you provide ongoing ROW maintenance in Texas?

We do — controlling regrowth so gas, power, and water corridors stay accessible, operable, and compliant year over year.

How much does right-of-way clearing cost?

ROW clearing is priced by scope and production — hourly, daily, by the acre, or lump sum — after we review the route.
